Getting here

A short walk from the Tuesday Market.

Blackfriars Street sits in the heart of historic King's Lynn — close to the Tuesday Market Place, the Custom House, and the river.

On foot from the Tuesday Market Place: head south down Blackfriars Street, we're a few doors down on the right. Number 10. The shop window will give us away.

Driving in: short-stay parking on Blackfriars Street and the surrounding streets, with longer-stay car parks at St James (PE30 5DG) and Baxters Plain (PE30 1JP), both within a five-minute walk.
