What we make

Services at Violets.

From a single wrapped bunch to the full breadth of a wedding day. Prices on request — every brief is different.

Bouquets & bunches

For Tuesdays, anniversaries, and getting back in her good books.

№ 01 Florist's choice bouquet Take home today

A hand-tied bunch from whatever's at its best on the bench. Pick your colour palette — bright, soft, autumnal, all-white. Wrapped in kraft, tied with twine, water bag for the journey.

№ 02 The "make it special" bunch A step up

More stems, the best of what's in. Garden roses if they're in, peonies in season, lily-of-the-valley when we can get it. Tell us the budget, we'll show you what it buys.

№ 03 A jug for the kitchen table Arrangement in a vessel

Built into a jug, vase, or vessel of your choice. Lives where you put it down — no faff. Lovely as a gift for someone who'd rather not arrange their own.

Weddings & events

Flowers for the day, the room, and the moment.

We sit down with you, look at your venue, talk about season and palette, and build the day from there. Quotes are bespoke — no fixed packages.

№ 04 Bridal & bridesmaid bouquets By appointment

Hand-tied to suit dress, shape, and season. Trial available on request.

№ 05 Buttonholes & corsages By the unit

For groom, groomsmen, fathers, ushers, and mothers of. Magnetic or pin fixing.

№ 06 Ceremony arrangements Set on the day

Aisle markers, altar arrangements, archway pieces, pew ends. Delivered, set, and collected.

№ 07 Table flowers Low, tall, or scattered

Low jug arrangements for talking across, tall pedestal pieces, or runners down the length of the long table. Your call.

Sympathy & funerals

Quiet, careful work, for the hard days.

Funeral tributes are made in-house, dressed with respect, and delivered to the funeral director or directly to the service.

№ 08 Coffin sprays Single or double

Long sprays for the top of the casket. Traditional or contemporary styling.

№ 09 Sheaves & posies From mourners

Tied sheaves to lay, or posies for the family. Cards written with care.

№ 10 Wreaths & lettering Made to order

Round wreaths, hearts, cushions, and floral lettering (MUM, DAD, NAN, names). Allow 48 hours where possible.

All year

Seasonal & gift.

Wreaths through the winter. Posies through spring. Dried bouquets and foliage pieces in between. Plants and gifts in the shop on the day.
