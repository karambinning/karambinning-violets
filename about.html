About the shop

Violets, of Blackfriars Street.

An independent florist in old King's Lynn, working with what's at market, the season we're in, and what suits the person we're tying it for.

The shop

A small bench, a sharp pair of scissors, and Norfolk light through the window.

Violets is a working flower shop, not a website that pretends to be one. The flowers you see are the flowers we have. The arrangements you order are tied that day, by hand, on the bench at the back.

We're on Blackfriars Street, in a quiet corner of old King's Lynn — close to the Tuesday Market and the river. People wander in for a bunch on the way home, ring ahead for arrangements they want collected, and book us for weddings, christenings, and the days no one wants to plan for.

Our preference is for British growers when the season is generous, and for varieties that smell like something — old garden roses, sweet peas in June, narcissi in February — rather than the long-haul cold-chain stems that all look the same in a vase.

How we work

No two arrangements out of the door are the same.

Tell us what it's for, when you need it, and who it's going to. We'll work to it.

i.
You tell us the occasion

Birthday, anniversary, get-well, just-because. Funeral, wedding, christening. The brief shapes the flowers.

ii.
We work to the season

Whatever's at market that morning. Substitutions happen. We won't fake a peony in October.

iii.
Tied, wrapped, handed over

Collected from the shop, or for larger arrangements, delivered locally on the day. Always in person, never courier-thrown.

A note

We'd rather take the time, and get it right.

Wedding consultations and large commissions are by appointment. For everything else, walk in, or call the shop and we'll take the brief over the phone.
