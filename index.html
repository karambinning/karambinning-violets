What we do

Flowers made by hand, not the click of a courier.

Every arrangement that leaves the shop is built on the bench that morning — chosen stem by stem, wrapped in paper, tied with twine.

Walk in for a bouquet to take home tonight. Ring ahead for an arrangement you'd like collected. For weddings, funerals, and larger commissions, we sit down with you and plan it properly — flowers, season, colour palette, where they need to be and when.

Our work changes with what's at market. Some weeks the focus is peonies and garden roses; other weeks it's ranunculus, anemones, or whatever the British growers have cut that morning. We build to the flowers we have, not to a stock photo.

Made for

Four reasons people walk in — and one of them is no reason at all.

01 The everyday bouquet Take it home tonight

A hand-tied bunch of whatever is best this week — pick your colour way, leave the rest to us. Wrapped in kraft, tied with twine.

02 Weddings & events Book a sit-down

Bouquets, buttonholes, table arrangements, ceremony pieces. We work to your colour palette, your season, and the room you're in.

03 Sympathy & funerals Discreet & respectful

Sheaves, sprays, casket flowers, and posies for those left behind. We'll handle it gently — call or come in.

04 Seasonal & gift All year

Wreaths through winter, posies through spring, foliage and dried pieces in between. Gift-wrap, message card included.
